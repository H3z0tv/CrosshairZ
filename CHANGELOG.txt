Crosshair Z - Changelog

1.0.0 - 2026-11-13
- Crosshair Z is rebuilt from the ground up: all new code, a new settings window and new images.
- Uses the Ace3 libraries (AceAddon, AceEvent, AceDB, AceConsole) for start-up, events, saved settings and slash commands. Settings are saved in a new file, so they start fresh once more.
- Fresh start: settings from earlier versions are not carried over, and the Setup Wizard runs on your first login. Your keys carry over.
- New names: Aim Mode (camera follows the mouse), Crosshair Aim (target what you aim at), Mouse Actions (clicks cast while Aim Mode is on), Auto Cursor (cursor comes back for game windows), Target Hold (keep a target while you aim elsewhere).
- Aim Mode is on when you log in. Turn that off with Aim Mode On At Login on the Aim Mode page.
- Turning Aim Mode off now also turns off aiming: the crosshair hides and aiming stops picking targets until you turn it back on.
- Camera works like before: over the shoulder with a strong pitch so your character sits below the crosshair, and the camera turns toward your target. All of it is on the Camera page, and Motion Sickness Protection turns it off.
- Class resource orbs fill from left to right, sit outside the arcs, and shrink together with the HUD so a small HUD stays tidy.
- DynamicCam: when it is installed, Crosshair Z leaves the whole camera to it (shoulder, pitch, zoom, scenes and turn speed) and says so once in chat.
- Tooltips during Aim Mode are rebuilt: no flicker, the chosen key shows and hides them right away, and they sit next to the crosshair.
- Move Prompts works again: the prompts stay under your mouse while you drag, sample prompts show while moving, and the saved place is right at any prompt size.
- Interaction prompt redone: a hopping bag when you can loot, a speech bubble with typing dots when you can talk, a turning gear for objects, and just your interact key on a key cap under it. The name is optional (Show Name).
- Arc Thickness is now a slider with six steps.
- Interaction prompt: a brush-stroke circle sweeps in around the icon and slowly turns while the prompt is up. Prompts fade in and out instead of popping.
- When Aim Mode turns off, the cursor comes back where you left it instead of the middle of the screen.
- Crosshair HUD redesigned: two crescents on each side close almost into a circle around the crosshair, thickest in the middle and tapering at the ends, with a dark rim so they read on any ground and a soft neon glow. Your health and power on the left, your target's health and cast on the right, health percent beside each side. Options: Arc Style (smooth or with quarter marks), Neon Glow, Show Percent, Arc Thickness.
- 24 new crosshair styles with a dark rim so they stay visible on any background. The crosshair now sits above your character by default.
- New settings window: groups along the top, pages under them, one column of settings, smooth scrolling, animated switches and sliders, five color themes and a window scale.
- New Target Hold page: hold key, switch target, cycle keys, auto hold, marker and optional sounds.
- New Camera Scenes page: pick a scene and edit its zoom, shoulder, head tracking and field of view in one place.
- New installs start with a ready-made setup: Aim Mode on, Bead crosshair a bit above your character, compact smooth HUD at 60% size with thick arcs, red orbs, tooltips beside the crosshair, vignette on, hit and kill markers off. Left Click runs a macro named TARGET when you have one, otherwise Action Button 1.
- Profiles can be shared: Export Profile shows a text string to copy, Import Profile reads one back as a new profile (Profiles page, or /cz export and /cz import).
- Every setting lives in a profile, including mouse actions, and profiles can follow your specialization.
- Undo All Changes puts back the game settings Crosshair Z changed, as they were before it first changed them.
- Type /cz status to see the version, whether Aim Mode is on, what paused it, and where the crosshair is.

0.1.8 - 2026-11-13
- Cycle Lock keys: two new keybinds, Cycle Lock - Next Enemy and Cycle Lock - Previous Enemy (Mouse Look page). They use the game's own enemy cycling, so they work in combat, and the enemy you cycle to is locked (turn that off with Cycle Keys Lock The New Target).
- Target Lock marker: the TARGET LOCKED tag now flashes and reads TARGET CASTING while your locked enemy is casting, and pulses softly every few seconds. Both can be turned off.
- Lock sounds: optional sounds for locking, unlocking, cycling and when your locked target starts a cast. Off by default. Pick each sound, the sound channel, and use Test Sounds to hear them.
- Auto Lock (off by default): locks an enemy when you land your first harmful spell on it.
- Undo All Changes and /czundo now put your game settings back to what you had before Crosshair Z, saved on a fresh install before anything is changed. If Crosshair Z was installed before this version, your own values were not saved and Blizzard defaults are used, as before.

0.1.7 - 2026-11-09
- Resource Orbs: the class resource dots around the reticle (combo points for Rogue and Druid, holy power, soul shards, chi, arcane charges, essence, runes) are now gold-ringed orbs. Same spot and same Placement, Distance, Spacing and Size controls as the old dots. All settings are on the Reticle HUD page under Class Resources, and the HUD preview shows the orbs.
- Animated: a new point pops in with a flash and a striped shine, spent points shrink away one after another, essence shows the next point charging, and a full set glows softly.
- The orbs are part of the Reticle HUD: they show, fade and dim with it, so Show HUD, HUD Opacity, HUD Size and Offset all apply, and they hide out of combat the same way the dots did. Choose red orbs or a color per resource.
- Turn Orb Style off to go back to the small dots. If you had the dots turned off, the orbs start off too.

0.1.6 - 2026-11-08
- Tooltips During Mouse Look (Mouse Look page): Hide, Show, or show only while holding Shift, Ctrl or Alt. When shown, you get the tooltip of whatever is under the crosshair.
- Tooltip Position: next to the crosshair or at the game's default tooltip position.

0.1.5 - 2026-11-08
- Fixed: the interaction prompt showed an empty badge on lootable corpses. Prompts now show Crosshair Z's own bag, speech bubble and gear icons in a flat teal style, without the ring and background; the glow is off by default.
- Setup Wizard on first install: Free Look key, crosshair style, HUD and camera in a few steps. Run it again any time with /cz wizard or the Setup Wizard button.
- What's New popup on the first login after an update.
- 25 new Crosshair Z crosshair styles and a style browser on the Crosshair page: click a style to use it.
- Crosshair states: dim or hide the crosshair while mounted, and hide or keep a faint crosshair while Free Look is off.
- Crosshair cast feedback: grows while you cast or channel, pulses when the cast lands, shakes when it's interrupted.
- Weapons can follow Free Look: drawn when it turns on, put away when it turns off (out of combat).
- Free Look vignette: darker screen edges while Free Look is on, with adjustable strength.
- Base Camera: field of view, max zoom distance, zoom scroll speed and head tracking strength.
- Motion Sickness Protection: Crosshair Z never moves the camera off-center or makes it follow head movement.
- Target Lock keybind: keeps your current target while you aim elsewhere. Choose Toggle or Switch Target behavior.
- Flip Camera keybind: moves the camera to your other shoulder.
- Unit Outlines option for the game's outline around mouseover, target and group units.
- Macro Composer: build a cast macro aimed at your crosshair target, mouseover, cursor, focus or yourself and save it in one click.

0.1.4 - 2026-11-07
- Interaction prompt shows a bag icon and Press F to loot on corpses you can loot. NPCs show Press F to talk, objects Press F to interact.

0.1.3 - 2026-11-07
- True Reticle is now Crosshair Z: new name, new logo, new commands /cz, /crosshairz and /czundo.
- Fixed: the suggested cast ignored Only In Combat and the Show Suggested Cast switch and stayed on screen.
- A PREVIEW label shows under the crosshair while the settings window is open, so preview elements aren't mistaken for real ones.
- Live editing: while the settings window is open, the crosshair, Reticle HUD and Assist HUD stay on screen with sample values, so every change shows the moment you make it. Hide rules like Only in combat pause while you edit.
- Preview box with the crosshair, arcs and Assist HUD prompts at the top of the Crosshair, Smart Crosshair, Reticle HUD and Assist HUD pages.
- The settings window opens on the left side so the middle of the screen stays visible.
- Text fields in the settings window now save when you click away, switch pages or close the window, not only on Enter. Esc still cancels.
- New Reticle HUD: arcs around the crosshair show your health and power on the left and your target's health and cast on the right. The target arc glows when an enemy is in execute range.
- HUD Size & Position: size from 25% to 200%, thin, normal or thick arcs, background ring strength, and horizontal and vertical offsets. Pips can sit below, above, left or right, with adjustable distance, spacing and size, and no longer overlap the arcs.
- Live preview of the crosshair, arcs and pips at the top of the HUD Size & Position settings.
- Show HUD: only in combat (default), in combat or with a target, or always.
- Class resource pips under the crosshair: combo points, holy power, soul shards, chi, arcane charges, essence and runes.
- Hit and kill feedback: a hit marker flashes on the crosshair when you deal damage (bigger and gold on crits), and a red marker with a glow when your enemy dies.
- New Assist HUD: the next spell the game suggests for your rotation (Assisted Combat) shows as an icon next to the crosshair with its keybind (or your mouse click while in Free Look), and an interaction prompt shows your Interact key and the name when the crosshair is on an NPC or object. Size, position, opacity and combat options for both.
- Interaction prompt now reads Press F to interact (with your key) under the name, glows softly, and the game shows the name above the NPC or object you can interact with.
- Assist HUD prompts can be moved: Move Prompts button or /cz move, drag them, then press Done.
- New installs start with the Dot crosshair and middle mouse button as the Free Look toggle (only if nothing else is bound to it).
- New Smart Crosshair: the crosshair turns green in melee range, yellow in ranged range and red when nothing reaches. Range spells are picked from your spellbook automatically, or you can set your own.
- Crosshair hide rules: show always, only in combat or only with an enemy target, and hide in flight form, when idle, without a target, on friendly or dead targets, or when out of range.
- Combat opacity: separate crosshair opacity in and out of combat.
- Head tracking: the crosshair follows your character's head height as you zoom in and out.
- Screen effects: purple screen edges while stealthed and red pulsing edges at low health.
- New Situational Camera (off by default): zoom, shoulder offset, head movement and field of view change by situation. 16 situations: dead, flight path, skyriding, NPC dialogue, PvP, boss, combat, dungeon, stealth, swimming, vehicle, mounted, sitting, fishing, indoors and world.
- Skyriding zoom grows with your speed, and the camera slowly orbits around you while you /sit, /kneel or /sleep.
- New Profiles: HUD, Smart Crosshair and Camera settings are saved in profiles. Create, copy, reset and delete profiles, and use a different profile per specialization.
- Preview buttons for the HUD, hit, crit and kill markers and the low health effect.
- Settings window: new HUD & Camera section, Profiles page, color pickers and a Profile card on Home.

0.1.1 - 2026-11-06
- New settings window (/cz): sidebar navigation, Home page with player card, status cards, quick actions and recent pages, Patch Notes page, theme picker and panel scale.
- Settings are split into Mouse Look, Reticle Targeting, Crosshair, Click Casting and Advanced pages. Reticle Targeting and Crosshair can be switched on or off right from the sidebar.
- Game Options > AddOns > Crosshair Z now has an Open Crosshair Z button.
- /cz followed by a page name opens that page directly, for example /cz crosshair.
- New option: Show Target Circle (Reticle Targeting, on by default). Turns the circle under your target back on at every login, including on WoW: Forever.
- New Crosshair Z logo (addon list icon and About page).
- Crosshair Z colors are now green to match the logo (title, Mouse Look headers and labels).

0.1.0 - 2026-11-06
- First release of Crosshair Z.
- Supports Retail Midnight (12.1.5) and WoW: Forever from one download.
- Free Look: turn the camera with the mouse without holding right click (toggle or hold keybind).
- Reticle Targeting: aim the crosshair at enemies to target them; @mouseover and @cursor macros work with the crosshair.
- Mouse Click Casting: free your mouse buttons to cast up to 8 abilities while Free Look is on.
- Cursor Unlock: the cursor is released automatically when bags, map, character panel and other windows open.
- Action Camera preset, shoulder offset, sticky crosshair and 13 crosshair styles.
- Crosshair colors are safe with Midnight secret values in dungeons, raids and combat.
- Vendor mount detection now works on every client language.
- Tooltip hiding during Free Look no longer replaces the game tooltip's own scripts.
- Commands: /cz or /crosshairz opens settings, /czundo reverts all changes and disables the addon.
- Keybinds are under Options > Keybindings > AddOns > Crosshair Z.
